# Privacy Policy — SendGrid Connector by IIInigence

_Effective: August 2026_

The SendGrid Connector is a local MCP server that runs entirely on your device inside Claude Desktop (or your MCP client).

## What we collect

**Nothing.** IIInigence collects no data from this connector. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no tracking, and it never communicates with IIInigence servers.

## Where your data goes

- **Your SendGrid API key** is stored by Claude Desktop on your device (using the operating system's secure storage for sensitive extension settings) and is sent only to SendGrid's API (`api.sendgrid.com`, or the regional endpoint you configure) to authenticate requests.
- **Email content, contact data, and statistics** flow directly between your device and SendGrid's API. No third party — including IIInigence — sits in that path.
- **Your AI assistant** (e.g. Claude) sees the tool inputs and outputs as part of your conversation, subject to the privacy policy of the AI product you use.

## Your controls

- **Read-only mode** blocks all sending and writing at the connector level.
- Remove the connector at any time via your client's extension settings; revoke the API key at any time in SendGrid.

## Third-party services

Use of SendGrid is governed by [Twilio's privacy policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy). Use of Claude is governed by [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
